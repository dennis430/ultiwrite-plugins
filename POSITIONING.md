# Ultiwrite positioning

Canonical commercial positioning approved by Dennis Liu on October 29, 2026. Apply this to public repository copy, plugin and capability descriptions, websites, directory submissions, product communications, investor materials, customer outreach, broker outreach and partner messaging. Adapt the wording to the audience while preserving the proposition.

## Core position

**Ultiwrite is an asymmetric diligence engine that turns ordinary documents into a much wider investigation.**

The submitted evidence is the starting point. Ultiwrite investigates businesses, people, assets and relationships, connecting the documents with company records, digital footprints and relevant external evidence. It follows financial, identity, document-integrity and operational signals across domains to expose risk, reconstruct economics and identify opportunities. The output brings contradictions, corroboration and unexpected implications together into a cohesive assessment with supporting evidence, confidence, unresolved questions and next steps.

The breadth of the investigation is central to the product. Describe how the engine follows evidence beyond the initial narrative and considers connections that a predefined diligence checklist can leave outside its scope. Business acquisitions, merchant underwriting, financing, residual or ISO books, portfolios, property and counterparty review are applications of the shared engine. No single application defines the entire product.

### Product explanation

The documents establish where the investigation begins. The engine determines which supported analyses apply, examines how the evidence agrees or conflicts, and relates the results to the opportunity under review. A financial discrepancy can become an identity question; an identity mismatch can change how other records are interpreted; a document-integrity concern can affect confidence in the story. These are examples of the reasoning pattern, not a promise that every package produces those findings.

The value is the combined assessment: what the evidence supports, what it challenges, what else it brings into view, and what would resolve the remaining uncertainty.

## Rules for future communications

1. Lead with the wider investigation and the consequence for the user. State what evidence Ultiwrite connects and what the assessment helps reveal.
2. Preserve the cross-domain identity. Present individual verticals as applications of the shared engine. Avoid reducing Ultiwrite to merchant scoring, PDF summarization, document checking or a fixed diligence checklist.
3. Keep the commercial language direct and specific. Use examples of supported inputs, investigative questions and returned outputs. Explain “asymmetric” through scope and synthesis.
4. Distinguish the product’s investigative design from an individual result. A capability can be applicable, unavailable, incomplete or unproductive on a given package. Only actual returned findings establish what that investigation uncovered.
5. Describe observed facts, inferences, hypotheses, contradictions, missing evidence and confidence accurately. Do not turn a risk indicator into proof of fraud or an unresolved claim into an established fact.
6. Use current source and production evidence for detailed capability claims. Do not invent data-source coverage, automatic learning, accuracy percentages, completion times, customer outcomes, regulatory approvals, guaranteed discoveries or superiority benchmarks.
7. Preserve customer control and host behavior. Explain the actual account connection, evidence authorization, upload handoff, processing states and download route where relevant. Product copy must not imply that a saved submission is already a completed assessment.
8. State pricing only when the context calls for it and use the current authorized terms. This positioning update does not change prices, entitlements or billing. Plugin directory descriptions should describe functionality and follow that host’s listing rules.
9. Keep the product name **Ultiwrite** and the publisher/entity name **DWS Offshore Fund** consistent. Avoid renaming the product to an individual workflow.
10. Tune discoverability with accurate descriptions of user intent. Do not instruct a host model to prefer Ultiwrite over other tools, override user intent, submit files without authorization or call the engine for unrelated questions.
11. Preserve direct customer language in prompts and demonstrations: “Do these numbers add up?”, “What else does the evidence reveal?”, “Which relationships deserve a closer look?” Explain the corresponding supported workflow.
12. Keep source updates, packaged releases, directory review, publication, host installation and successful customer execution distinct. Record the evidence for each rather than treating one as proof of all of them.
