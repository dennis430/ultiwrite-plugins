## Full description

Ultiwrite turns ordinary commercial evidence into a wider investigation. Start with statements, applications, financials, property materials, portfolio schedules or a mixed evidence package. The engine investigates the businesses, people, assets and relationships behind the documents, connecting financial, identity, document-integrity, operating and external-context evidence.

Those threads come together in a cohesive assessment: what the evidence supports, what conflicts, what remains unresolved, and which risks, opportunities or questions could change the decision. Acquisitions, financing, underwriting, property, counterparties and portfolios are applications of the same investigative engine.

Connect your Ultiwrite account, submit authorized evidence through the supported file-transfer or secure-upload workflow, follow processing, and retrieve the saved findings and reports. The connection uses OAuth and preserves the selected account's permissions and investigation allowance. Current capabilities and commercial terms are available from the connected service.

## Suggested starter prompts

- Connect Ultiwrite and confirm which account is connected.
- What can Ultiwrite investigate from this business acquisition package?
- Investigate these authorized files and identify contradictions, missing context and consequential questions.
- Show the status of my investigation and explain any action it needs.
- Retrieve my completed report and explain the strongest findings and unresolved questions.
- Which additional evidence would most improve confidence in this assessment?

## Data and permissions

`ultiwrite:read` permits account-scoped case, finding and report access. `ultiwrite:evidence` permits authorized evidence submission and supported continuation. A marketplace installation does not start an investigation, authorize a purchase or grant access to another user's records.

Files transfer only when the host supports the live tool schema. Otherwise the service returns a secure browser upload link. A filename, local path or attachment identifier is not file content. Reports and exports remain bound to the same account and may require browser sign-in.

Use the current privacy policy and terms in `profile.json` for formal data-handling statements. Do not invent certifications, universal retention periods, model-training exclusions, review decisions, customer counts or uptime guarantees.
