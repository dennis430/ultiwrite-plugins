## Product truth
Ultiwrite is **Asymmetric Diligence & Investigative Intelligence**. The submitted documents and data are the starting point, not the investigation boundary. The engine identifies relevant leads, investigates beyond the initial evidence, expands the evidentiary foundation, and reasons across the combined corpus. It connects relationships, reconciles contradictions and corroborating evidence, and reconstructs the most strongly supported account of the situation. This enables findings, conclusions and verification the original submissions alone could not support.

Evidence expansion is not fabrication. Distinguish sourced observations, derived relationships, analytical inference, confidence and unresolved claims. Commercial diligence, underwriting, merchant risk, document forensics and other existing use cases are applications of the engine, not its entire identity. Do not reveal investigative heuristics, scoring weights or proprietary orchestration.
