## Long description

Bring Ultiwrite's asymmetric diligence and investigative intelligence into Microsoft 365 Copilot.

Connect your Ultiwrite account to find saved commercial diligence cases and reports, read evidence-backed findings, explain decision briefs, and open available report downloads. Keep the report's saved decision, confidence, severity, and evidence references intact while discussing what the documents show, what was inferred, and what remains unresolved.

Start a new investigation with evidence you are authorized to share. Ultiwrite provides a secure browser upload link for your documents, spreadsheets, images, or other currently supported inputs. Return after uploading to check the matching case and read the resulting report when it is ready. The service reports current accepted inputs and account limits.

Useful starting prompts:

- “Connect Ultiwrite and confirm which account is connected.”
- “Show my recent Ultiwrite cases and saved reports.”
- “Help me submit commercial evidence that I am authorized to share.”
- “Explain the saved decision, confidence, evidence, and unresolved questions in this report.”
- “Check whether my case's report is ready.”
- “Open the available downloads for this report.”

An Ultiwrite account and approved service permissions are required. Access and usage eligibility depend on your Ultiwrite account. Microsoft 365 availability depends on your organization's Microsoft entitlement and administrator policies. The connector package does not include a service subscription.

Files are uploaded in your browser; attaching a document to Copilot does not automatically send it to Ultiwrite. Report downloads also open in your browser under the same Ultiwrite account. The agent explains the saved report and does not change the service's decision or findings.
